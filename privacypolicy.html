Privacy Policy

How SoftSin Studios handles privacy.

This page explains what information may be collected when you use SoftSin Studios pages, tools, links, and the message board.

Last updated: April 30, 2026

Plain-English summary

SoftSin Studios is built around public creator tools, documentation, artwork, and community posts. The browser-based tools are designed to run locally in your browser unless a page clearly uses an account, external service, or linked platform.

Information you provide

You may provide information when you create a message board account, post content, request support, follow external links, or use support platforms connected to SoftSin Studios.

  • Message board profile details, such as display name, avatar, or account information provided through sign-in.
  • Posts, replies, images, links, bug reports, feedback, or other content you choose to submit.
  • Support messages or contact details you choose to provide when asking for help.

Message board and account sign-in

The SoftSin Studios message board may use third-party authentication and database services, including Discord OAuth and Supabase. These services may process account/session information required to sign in, maintain a session, display profiles, store posts, and operate the board.

Do not post private, sensitive, financial, medical, legal, or account-security information on the message board.

Browser tools and local files

SoftSin Studios tools may let you load images, JSON files, text, prompt data, or workflow-related files into your browser. Unless a tool clearly states otherwise, these files are handled in your browser for the purpose of using the tool.

Some tools may export files, PNGs, JSON, or text using the data you entered. You are responsible for reviewing anything you download, publish, share, or use in other software.

Cookies, sessions, and local storage

The site may use cookies, browser storage, or session tokens when needed for sign-in, message board access, preferences, security, or normal site operation.

External links

SoftSin Studios links to external platforms such as social sites, gallery pages, support pages, and other services. Those platforms have their own privacy policies and data practices. SoftSin Studios does not control those external services.

Analytics and logs

Hosting providers, security systems, or third-party services may automatically collect basic technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, referring pages, and request logs. This information may be used for security, diagnostics, reliability, and abuse prevention.

How information may be used

Information may be used to operate the site, provide message board features, respond to support requests, improve tools, prevent spam or abuse, moderate community areas, and maintain site security.

User-generated content

Content you post publicly on the message board or other public areas may be visible to other users. Public posts should be treated as public information.

Age restriction

SoftSin Studios is not intended for anyone under 18. Do not use the message board or submit personal information if you are under 18.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as SoftSin Studios changes, adds tools, updates services, or changes hosting/account systems. The updated date at the top of this page will be changed when the policy is revised.

Contact and support

For questions, support, bug reports, or privacy-related requests, use the SoftSin Studios message board or the official support links provided on the website.
