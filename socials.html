About

Solo-built creator tools, rendered work, and workflow experiments.

SoftSin Studios is a solo creative project focused on practical tools for AI art, pose control, prompt structure, ComfyUI workflows, rendered images, and music-adjacent creative experiments.

The goal is simple: build tools that remove friction instead of adding another blinking cockpit to the pile. If a tool helps with pose construction, prompt cleanup, metadata, posting, or repeatable workflow structure, it belongs here.
